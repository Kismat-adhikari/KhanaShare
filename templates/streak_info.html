How It Works

Your streak increases by 1 each day you log in consecutively. Miss a day, and your streak resets to 1. The higher your streak, the more intense your flame becomes!

Keep The Streak

To maintain your streak, make sure to log in at least once every 24 hours. Your streak will reset if you miss a day, so stay consistent!
